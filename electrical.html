Testing & compliance

Helping systems remain safe and dependable.

  • Electrical Installation Condition Reports (EICRs)
    Read more

    J.L. Clark & Son Ltd has extensive experience carrying out Electrical Installation Condition Reports across commercial and industrial installations. Our inspections are undertaken thoroughly and methodically, drawing on many years of practical experience working with complex electrical systems.

    We place particular emphasis on the quality of both the inspection and the finished report. Observations are clearly recorded, appropriately classified and supported by detailed information to give the client a clear understanding of the condition of their electrical installation.

    Where defects or areas requiring attention are identified, we provide practical information to assist with any necessary remedial works. Our aim is to provide a detailed, accurate and high-quality EICR, rather than simply completing an inspection to satisfy a compliance requirement.

    Where required, inspections can be arranged around operational requirements, including evening and out-of-hours working, helping minimise disruption to the customer's business.

  • Portable Appliance Testing (PAT)
    Read more

    J.L. Clark & Son Ltd provides reliable Portable Appliance Testing for commercial and industrial customers, from smaller premises through to sites with large quantities of equipment.

    Equipment is carefully inspected and tested, with clear identification and detailed records maintained. We can also manage scheduled repeat testing to help keep ongoing requirements up to date.

    Flexible and out-of-hours testing can be arranged where required, allowing work to be completed around the customer's operating hours with minimal disruption.

    Practical advice, fault diagnosis and repair work can also be provided where required.
